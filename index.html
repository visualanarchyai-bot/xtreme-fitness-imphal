HTML-native video mockups you can run for free

FrameForge is a lightweight, static HyperFrames-style playground. Compose scenes with HTML, CSS, and timing attributes, preview them instantly, and record the animated preview in your browser.

composition.html
<section data-duration="6" class="scene">
  <h1 data-start="0.4" data-end="3.5">Launch day</h1>
  <p data-start="1.1" data-end="5">Animate with plain CSS</p>
</section>

How to use it

  1. Write HTMLDescribe each scene as normal markup with classes and timing attributes.
  2. Style motionAdd CSS keyframes or inline scripts that are safe to replay in the iframe preview.
  3. Preview locallyFrameForge injects your composition into an isolated stage and loops the timeline.
  4. Record outputUse the built-in recorder for a quick WebM, or capture the stage with your favorite free screen recorder.
